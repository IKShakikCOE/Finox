# 🎯 Finox — Product Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
**Finox** is an all-in-one personal finance and wealth management platform specifically designed for the **Bangladesh** economic market. While existing global personal finance apps (e.g., Mint, Toshl, YNAB, Wallet) cater to Western banking and taxation, they completely fail to model Bangladeshi realities—such as **National Savings Certificates (সঞ্চয়পত্র)**, **DPS/FDR schemes**, **NBR progressive income tax & rebate rules**, **General Provident Fund (GPF)**, and **bKash/Nagad mobile banking accounts**.

Finox unifies daily expense tracking with institutional intelligence (bank, insurance, and mutual fund comparisons) and wealth building tools into a single, cohesive super-app.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Bangladeshi earners, professionals, and small business owners face fragmented financial lives:
1. **Scattered Money**: Money is distributed across cash, mobile wallets (bKash, Nagad), and multiple banks (Dutch-Bangla, BRAC, City Bank).
2. **Hidden Wealth Data**: Long-term investments in Sanchayapatra or bank DPS/FDRs are tracked on paper or forgotten in passbooks.
3. **Tax Guesswork**: Filing annual income tax returns to the National Board of Revenue (NBR) is confusing, resulting in missed tax rebates from eligible investments.
4. **Information Asymmetry**: Finding the best deposit rates or insurance claim settlement ratios requires manually browsing dozens of archaic bank and insurance websites.

---

## 3. Product Vision & Goals
* **Unified Financial Dashboard**: Instantly view total net worth, liquid cash, monthly cash flow, and asset allocation in BDT (৳).
* **Automated & Manual Transaction Ledger**: Effortless daily income and expense tracking with category budgets, debt management, and subscriptions.
* **Bangladeshi Wealth Engine**: Accurate mathematical modeling of Sanchayapatra profits, DPS maturity forecasts, and GPF balances.
* **Statutory Compliance**: Built-in NBR tax calculator with slab-by-slab tax liability and investment tax rebate certificates.
* **Institutional Catalog & Web Crawler**: Automated crawling of bank and AMC rates to empower users with side-by-side product comparisons.

## 4. Target User Personas

| Persona | Description | Key Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Salaried Professional** | Corporate / IT / Private sector employees in Dhaka/Chittagong | Salary breakdown, monthly take-home, NBR tax rebate, subscription tracking, and budget alerts. |
| **Prudent Investor / Retiree** | Individuals with savings in government schemes and fixed deposits | Sanchayapatra monthly profit tracking, FDR auto-renewal, and low-risk bank ratings. |

### Module Breakdown
1. **Dashboard**: High-level financial cockpit with 11 responsive KPI and analytical widgets.
2. **Tracker**: Daily transactions, multi-account balances (Cash, Bank, MFS), hierarchical categories, rollover budgeting, recurring transactions, subscription tracker, and goals.
3. **Notes & Debts**: Money lent (*পাওনা*) vs money borrowed (*দেনা*) tracker with settlement logs and financial note-taking.
4. **Income & Career**: Multi-stream earnings hub (agriculture, rentals, salary, investments) and career progression salary timeline.
5. **Tools (NBR Tax & Salary)**: Progressive income tax calculator, tax rebate optimizer, certificate generator, and take-home pay planner.
6. **Portfolio**: Sanchayapatra holdings, DPS/FDR lifecycle management, and Provident Fund balance.
7. **Institutional Catalog (Bank, Insurance, Mutual Funds)**: Profiles, credit ratings, product catalogs, and side-by-side comparison matrices.
8. **Investment / Ads**: Marketing campaign ROAS and conversion metrics.
9. **AI Financial Advisor**: Contextual conversational chat powered by user's financial ledger.
10. **Calendar & Reminders**: Due dates, bills, and tax deadlines.
11. **Internal Messaging & Notifications**: User communication and priority notification center.
12. **Administration**: Comprehensive catalog CRUD, Keycloak user management, audit logs, and seed data.
13. **Crawler Engine**: Automated scraping scheduler for scraping bank rates and insurance terms.
